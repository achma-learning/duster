# Duster mods — Morocco edition

Mods and upgrades for **this car**: 2016 Dacia Duster mk1 phase 2, **diesel 1.5 dCi (K9K), 4x2**, driven in **Morocco**
(heat 45 °C+, dust/sand, coastal salt, Atlas snow, heavy rain episodes, pistes).
Shopping assumption: **Temu** for cheap, non-safety accessories; local shops for everything safety-critical.

> Items marked *(check)* were not verified for this exact car — confirm in the owner's manual
> ([`manuals/`](manuals/)), the door-frame sticker, or by pulling the part before buying.

> ### ⭐ Best value / ROI — spend here first
> 1. **Maintenance** (§1.1–1.2): timing belt, fluids, filters, battery, diesel fuel filter. Cheapest way to avoid a
>    ruined engine or a breakdown in the middle of nowhere.
> 2. **Michelin Primacy 4+** (default choice, §1.3) **+ wheel alignment** at fitting: safer braking, less noise,
>    even tyre wear, lower fuel use.
> 3. **A good head unit — only if the car doesn't already have one** (§7.1): navigation, CarPlay/Android Auto, reverse
>    camera. *This car already has one* — see [`../headunit/`](../headunit/).
>
> Everything else (seats, cosmetics, lifestyle) comes after these.

## 1. Essential (do first)

### 1.1 Maintenance before mods — local parts only (Valeo, Bosch, Gates, SKF, Renault)
- **Timing belt kit + water pump** if no proof it was done (~120 000 km / 5 years, less in harsh use *(check manual)*). A snapped belt kills the K9K.
- **Coolant** (Renault Type D), clean radiator fins of dust/insects, check the fan.
- **AC service**: regas (R134a), leak check.
- **Battery**: heat kills batteries — quality **EFB/AGM** (Varta, Bosch, Exide), test every spring.
- **Oil**: diesel 5W-40, see §1.2. Shorter intervals in dust/heat.
- **Filters**: engine air filter checked every oil change; **carbon cabin filter** every 6–12 months.
- Brakes: front pads/discs, rear drums, fluid.

### 1.2 Diesel-specific (1.5 dCi)
- **Fuel filter** with water separator: replace on schedule *(check manual, often ~30 000–60 000 km)* and
  **drain water** at each service — the K9K common-rail injectors (Delphi/Continental) hate water and dirt.
  Genuine or Mann/Bosch/Purflux only.
- **Fuel**: fill at major stations (Afriquia, Shell, TotalEnergies…), avoid small rural pumps and jerrycan fuel.
  Never run the tank to empty (pulls sediment, air in the system).
- **Glow plugs**: check before winter if you go to the Atlas (hard cold starts, white smoke = suspect).
- **EGR / DPF** *(check if DPF fitted)*: city-only use clogs them — do a 20–30 min highway run every week or two.
  Don't remove/delete them (illegal, fails inspection). An occasional quality diesel cleaner (Liqui Moly, Wynn's) is OK.
- **Turbo care**: let it idle 30–60 s before switching off after highway, pistes or climbs in summer heat;
  don't rev hard when cold. Oil to spec matters most for the turbo.
- **Oil**: diesel spec **RN0710**, or **RN0720 if DPF** *(check)*, 5W-40. Change on time — diesel oil gets sooty.
- Watch for: smoke, loss of power ("limp mode"), injector rattle, oil leaks at the turbo hoses.
- No AdBlue on this generation.

### 1.3 Tyres — Michelin, 215/65 R16 (never from Temu)

| Use | Model |
|---|---|
| **⭐ Default — city + highway, heat** | **Michelin Primacy 4+** — heat-stable, quiet, good wet braking, long life |
| Frequent Atlas winter / lots of rain | **Michelin CrossClimate 2 (SUV)** — 3PMSF snow mark |
| Lots of pistes | **BFGoodrich All-Terrain KO2/KO3** (Michelin group) or **Michelin LTX Force** if available |

- **DOT date < 1–2 years** (heat ages stored stock), load/speed index ≥ door sticker.
- Check pressure **cold, in the morning**.
- **Alignment (parallélisme) every time you fit tyres** — and after hitting a big pothole or if the wheel pulls.
  Cheap, and protects the investment in Michelins.
- Balancing at fitting; rotate front ↔ rear every ~10 000 km.

### 1.4 Metal sump guard
Steel or 4–5 mm aluminium, Duster-specific, from a local fitter or known brand. Most important piste mod.

### 1.5 Headlights — the honest LED answer
1. **Polish yellowed lenses** first (biggest gain).
2. **Upgraded H4 halogen**: Philips X-tremeVision / Racing Vision, Osram Night Breaker — legal, 5-minute swap.
3. Branded LED with a fitment list (Philips Ultinon, Osram Night Breaker LED) — Duster mk1 often not listed, check.
4. Cheap Temu H4 LED: glare, no cut-off, risk at **visite technique** / police checks. Not recommended.

LED is fine for **fog, reverse, number plate and interior** bulbs.

### 1.6 Safety kit in the car
Triangle, extinguisher, first-aid kit, tyre gauge, 12 V compressor, tow strap (rated brand), 5–10 L water,
full-size spare checked. Jump starter: known brand, and **not left in the car in summer**.

## 6. Dashcam

### Must-haves for Morocco
| Feature | Why |
|---|---|
| **Supercapacitor** (no battery) | Battery swells / fire risk in summer heat — #1 rule |
| Sony STARVIS sensor (IMX335/415/675) | Readable plates, night video |
| Front 2K/4K + **rear 1080p** | Rear-end hits are common in city traffic |
| **Adhesive** mount | Suction cups drop in heat and on pistes |
| CPL filter (optional) | Cuts dashboard reflection in strong sun |
| GPS, Wi-Fi app | Speed/location evidence, easy clip download |
| Parking mode (optional) | Needs hardwire kit |

### Models
| Budget | Model |
|---|---|
| Cheap & good | **70mai A510** (front + rear) or 70mai A400 + RC09 |
| **Best value — recommended** | **VIOFO A229 Plus / A229 Pro 2CH** + CPL |
| Small, front only | VIOFO A119 Mini 2 |

Avoid no-name "4K dual lens" Temu cams (fake resolution, lithium battery, unreadable plates). On Temu, only known
brands from well-rated sellers, with "supercapacitor" in the specs.

**Memory card:** Samsung PRO Endurance or SanDisk High Endurance, 64–128 GB, from a trusted shop (fakes are common).

### Install in the Duster
1. Mount behind the rear-view mirror, inside the wiper sweep.
2. Simple power: 12 V socket (usually ignition-switched *(check)*).
3. Clean power: **hardwire kit + add-a-fuse** in the fuse box (check fuse type: mini vs low-profile); low-voltage
   cut-off **12.2–12.4 V** so parking mode doesn't flatten the battery in summer.
4. Rear cam: cable along the headliner and rear pillar to the top of the rear window (~1 h; trim tools ✅ Temu).

**Head-unit USB DVR cams** (see [`../headunit/`](../headunit/)): cheap but record only while the unit is on, poor
image, lost on crash/reboot — not a real dashcam.

**Legal / privacy:** dashcams are commonly used *(check current rules)*; under law **09-08**, don't publish faces or
plates — keep clips for police/insurance.

**Pick:** VIOFO A229 Plus 2CH + CPL + hardwire kit + Samsung PRO Endurance 128 GB.

## 7. Comfort & tech

### 7.1 Head unit (⭐ high ROI — only if the car has none)
This car **already has** an Android unit (Allwinner T3, see [`../headunit/`](../headunit/)) — skip unless it dies or
you want an upgrade. If buying one:
- **Real** specs: ≥ 4 GB RAM / 64 GB, 8-core, Android 12+ (fake-spec units claim 4 GB but have 1–2 GB — check reviews).
- **Wireless CarPlay / Android Auto**, IPS or QLED 9″ screen, DSP audio, reverse-camera input.
- **Duster 2010–2017 frame + CANbus / steering-wheel-control adapter** included.
- Known brands (e.g. Teyes, Joying) > no-name. Temu possible but read reviews from Duster owners.

### 7.2 Seats — better quality & cushioning
Duster mk1 seats are thin and the driver's bolster/foam often collapses with age. Options by value:

| Option | Notes | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Re-foam + reupholster** (⭐ best value) | A Moroccan *tapissier / sellier* replaces the tired foam with denser foam, adds lumbar support, new breathable fabric. Keeps original rails and airbags. | Local upholsterer |
| **Higher-trim Duster mk1 seats** (Prestige/Laureate: height adjust, better bolsters) | Same rails, bolts straight in. ⚠️ Seats with **side airbags** must have the connector plugged in, or the airbag light comes on and fails inspection | Casse (scrapyard) / used parts |
| Memory-foam cushion + lumbar pillow | Cheap quick fix; choose breathable/mesh for summer | ✅ Temu |
| **12 V ventilated seat cover** (fans) | Very useful in Moroccan heat | ✅ Temu (check plug fuse/amps) |
| Aftermarket bucket / sport seats | ❌ Not recommended: rails, airbags, seat-belt sensors, homologation, worse for long trips | — |
| Duster 2 / other-model seats | Possible but needs custom brackets & airbag wiring — garage job only | Specialist |

Never buy seat frames, rails or belt parts on Temu.

## Temu — buy / careful / never

- ✅ **OK:** mats, boot tray, seat covers, sun shade, armrest, mud flaps, phone mounts, chargers, interior/plate/fog
  LED bulbs, reverse camera, TPMS, compressor, gauge, recovery boards, shovel, butyl mats, trim tools, cleaning kit,
  snow socks, cosmetics.
- ⚠️ **Careful:** H4 headlight LED, dashcams (known brands only), compressors / jump starters (inflated specs).
- ❌ **Never:** tyres, brakes, suspension, timing belt kits, filters, tow bars, roof bars for real loads, recovery
  straps/hooks, non-Duster-specific skid plates.
