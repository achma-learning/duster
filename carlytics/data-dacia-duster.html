Pre-Purchase Inspection Checklist
33 checks

Use this checklist when you go to inspect the vehicle in person. Every check that reveals a problem is a negotiation point — or a reason to walk away.

Exterior6 items
  • Walk around the car in daylight — look for dents, scratches, and rust spots
  • Check that paint colour and finish match on every panel (mismatches suggest respray / accident repair)
  • Inspect panel gaps — uneven gaps indicate poor bodywork or frame damage
  • Open and close every door, the bonnet, and the boot — they should latch smoothly
  • Look for chips or cracks in all glass (windshield, side windows, rear window)
  • Check all lights: headlights, tail lights, indicators, fog lights, and brake lights
Interior6 items
  • Sniff for musty or mouldy smell (signs of flood damage or water leaks)
  • Check upholstery for tears, stains, and wear inconsistent with stated mileage
  • Test all seat adjustments (power and manual), mirrors, and window switches
  • Verify A/C blows cold and heater blows hot — test every fan speed
  • Check dashboard: no warning lights illuminated, all gauges functional
  • Inspect under floor mats and in the boot for water stains, silt, or rust on seat rail bolts
Under the Hood6 items
  • Check engine oil level and colour — milky oil may indicate head-gasket failure
  • Inspect coolant level and condition (no discolouration or oily residue)
  • Look for visible fluid leaks under the car and around the engine bay
  • Examine belts for cracks, fraying, or glazing
  • Check hoses for brittleness, swelling, or soft spots
  • Inspect battery terminals for corrosion and verify the battery holds a charge
Test Drive6 items
  • Cold start: engine should fire up smoothly without excessive smoke or noise
  • Accelerate onto a fast road — check for hesitation, misfires, or loss of power
  • Brake firmly — pedal should feel solid, no pulsing, pulling, or grinding
  • Drive at highway speed — no vibration in the steering wheel or seats
  • Listen for unusual noises: clicks, clunks, whines, or rattles from suspension and drivetrain
  • Transmission shifts smoothly through all gears (auto and manual); clutch engages cleanly
Documents4 items
  • Confirm the VIN on the dashboard and door jamb matches the registration document
  • Verify the title is clean — not salvage, rebuilt, or flood-branded
  • Check that the seller's name matches the registration certificate
  • Review the full service booklet — look for regular stamps and consistent mileage entries
History Verification5 items
  • Run a VIN history check — compare mileage records against the odometer reading
  • Check for open safety recalls that have not yet been remedied
  • Verify accident and damage history — does the seller's disclosure match the records?
  • Confirm the car is not listed in any stolen-vehicle database
  • Cross-check import history — know which countries the vehicle has been registered in
